# 3Dschool

3Dschool is a modified version of **Sawhorse** (https://github.com/sawhorse3d/sawhorse), adapted
for classroom use at the Scuola Secondaria di I grado R. Montecuccoli, Pavullo nel Frignano
(Italy).

What changed from Sawhorse:

- sign-in with the school's Google account (only `@smpavullo.com` addresses are accepted);
- every saved or exported file goes to the person's Google Drive, in a folder called "3D", and
  Open from Drive lists it (a browser download is the fallback);
- the interface in Italian, with English one click away;
- an About window with who adapted it and where it comes from;
- the project's own publishing workflow, replaced by one that publishes only this app.
